Quick Answers

Questions? We've Got You

The things people ask us most before booking.

How do I book a tour?
Send us a message on WhatsApp or use the form above. Tell us the tour, the date and the number of guests, and we'll confirm the details with you.
Is hotel pickup included?
Yes. Hotel and metro pickup is included with our tours.
Are the tours suitable for kids and families?
Yes, our tours are family-friendly and suitable for all ages.
Shared tour or private tour?
We offer both. Shared tours are the budget-friendly option, and private tours are just for your group. The Khorfakkan tour is private only.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. We keep our pricing transparent, with no hidden fees.
