Quick Answers

Questions? We've Got You

The things people ask us most before booking.

How do I book a tour?
Open the tour you want and press Book Now. Choose your date, pickup point and number of guests, and send it. You get a booking reference straight away, and our team confirms on WhatsApp. You pay cash on pickup, nothing online.
Is pickup included?
Yes, pickup and drop-off are free anywhere in Dubai. Sharing tours pick up from set points (metro stations and meeting points). Private tours pick you up from your hotel. Pickup from other emirates costs extra.
Are the tours suitable for kids and families?
Yes, our tours are family-friendly and suitable for all ages.
Shared tour or private tour?
We offer both. Shared tours are the budget-friendly option, and private tours are just for your group. The Khorfakkan tour is private only.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. We keep our pricing transparent, with no hidden fees.
