Our Story

A Modern Tour Operator, Born in Dubai

NXT Tours is a Dubai-based tour operator offering premium shared and private experiences across the UAE. We pair an experienced local team with carefully crafted itineraries to deliver tours that feel effortless, exciting, and genuinely memorable.

Whether it's the iconic skyline of Dubai, the cultural depth of Abu Dhabi, the mountain serenity of Hatta, or the hidden beauty of Khorfakkan — our mission is simple: show you the UAE the way it should be seen.
